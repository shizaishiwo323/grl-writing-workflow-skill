# Cases: Narrative Echo and Payoff

## Case 1: Introduction Promise Paid Off By MVC

Introduction promise:

Existing NMR interpretation mainly describes apparent pore-size or porosity changes, but lacks a dynamic NMR-interpretable metric that distinguishes channeling from wormholing by tracking matrix-vug coupling.

Results payoff:

Section 3.3 defines MVC and links earlier matrix-vug coupling to more efficient permeability breakthrough.

Why it works:

MVC is not introduced as an isolated metric. It resolves a gap prepared in the Introduction.

Transferable rule:

Do not introduce a new metric only in Results. First prepare why such a metric is needed in Introduction or in the preceding Results subsection.

## Case 2: T2 Pathways Motivate Hydraulic Validation

Earlier Results:

Section 3.1 identifies regime-specific T2 pathways.

Forward pointer:

These spectral pathways motivate testing whether NMR-inferred coupling corresponds to independent hydraulic flow-path optimization.

Later payoff:

Section 3.2 uses tortuosity evolution as hydraulic validation.

Thread summary:

Together, T2 evolution and tortuosity show that the regimes differ not only in geometry, but also in the timing of pore coupling and flow-path optimization.

Transferable rule:

When one Results subsection interprets a signal, the next subsection can test or validate that interpretation using an independent metric.
